Fluent Bit is the vendor-neutral (CNCF graduated) log shipper. Chosen
over Grafana Alloy so one DaemonSet can fan out to both Loki and (later)
Elasticsearch for EFK.

To bump: pull the new version, then VERIFY PROVENANCE before trusting
it — confirm the pulled tarball's hash matches upstream's published
digest for that version:
  shasum -a 256 <tgz>
  curl -s https://fluent.github.io/helm-charts/index.yaml \
    | awk '/^  [a-z0-9-]+:$/{c=($0=="  fluent-bit:")} c&&/digest:/{d=$2} c&&/version: <NEWVER>$/{print d; exit}'
The two must match. Then regenerate the .sha256, update CHART_VERSION in
../install.sh, delete the old tgz + sha256, update this file. Review the
chart changelog for breaking changes first.
